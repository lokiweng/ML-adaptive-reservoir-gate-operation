Original figure & replay method

The embedded figure is the saved v4 output for the storm with the largest rule-curve peak. Its orange line shows the tuned PPO policy trained with seed 42.

The rule curve was replayed with the notebook’s simulator. For PPO, gate positions were read from the saved figure and passed through the same simulator. PPO values and their running sums are approximate. The 30-storm results use the notebook’s reported means.

The waterline represents stored volume schematically. Storage is shown at the end of the selected hour. Spill is calculated from storage at the start of that hour. Downstream damage also includes local tributary inflow. MCM means million cubic metres. $M means millions of dollars.
